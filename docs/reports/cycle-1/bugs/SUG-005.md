# [SUGGESTION] Add site search

## Summary

The site has no search. The header has an empty search container but no search box.

## Current behaviour

No way to search across 22 streams (growing every week), 5 speakers, 2 posts and 4 wiki pages, other than browsing the lists.

## Suggested behaviour

Enable VitePress's built-in local search (`themeConfig.search.provider: 'local'`), with UA and EN locales. It needs no external service.

## Why it matters

Finding "the stream about OAuth" or "the one with the RAG demo" currently means scanning the streams table. The archive grows weekly.

## Source

- Found by: exploratory session "global UI", 2026-10-07 (also resolves the open question in requirements v1.0)

## If accepted

Proposed requirement for v1.1: REQ-024: The site has a search that finds streams, speakers and posts by title, in both languages.
