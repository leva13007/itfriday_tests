# [SUGGESTION] Stream pages: optimise cover images

## Summary

Stream pages load a ~2 MB PNG cover. On mobile, Lighthouse measures an LCP of 11+ s and a layout shift of ~0.21. Both are in the "poor" / "needs improvement" range of Core Web Vitals. REQ-015 has no threshold, so this is a suggestion, not a defect.

## Current behaviour

From the [cycle 1 Lighthouse baseline](../cycles/2026-10-cycle-1/lighthouse.md) (2026-10-07, deploy `03ab24e`):

| Page | Device | Perf | LCP | CLS | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/streams/022` | mobile | 64 | 11.5 s | 0.216 | 2117 KiB |
| `/streams/022` | desktop | 89 | 2.0 s | 0.09 | 2134 KiB |
| `/streams/` (for comparison) | mobile | 99 | 1.7 s | 0.054 | 211 KiB |

- Covers are 1.3–2.6 MB PNGs, 1672 px wide, shown at ~690 px
- The cover `<img>` has no `width` / `height` attributes
- One speaker photo (`/speakers/oleh-levchenko.jpg`) is 1.1 MB for a 120 px avatar

## Suggested behaviour

- Serve covers as WebP or AVIF at display size (e.g. 1380 px for 2× screens). Typically 100–200 KB instead of ~2 MB
- Add `width` and `height` to cover images, so space is reserved before they load (fixes CLS)
- Resize speaker photos to ~240 px (2× of 120 px)

## Why it matters

Stream pages are what people open from Telegram and YouTube links, usually on a phone. An 11-second LCP on mobile means the main image appears long after the page does, and the text jumps when it loads.
