The schedule page says the date and topic of the next stream will be announced in Telegram. At the same time the site already announces stream #022 for 9 Oct 2026 with a topic, guest and scheduled YouTube live.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open https://itfriday.community/schedule and read "Наступний стрім".
2. Open https://itfriday.community/streams/ and look at the first row.

## Expected result

The schedule page shows the next announced stream (#022, 09.10.2026, "AI пише код. Хто отримує підвищення?", guest Сергій Литвин), or at least links to it.

## Actual result

"Наступний стрім: Дата і тема будуть оголошені в Telegram." The page doesn't mention #022.
